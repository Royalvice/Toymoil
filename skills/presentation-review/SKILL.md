# Presentation Review

Read docs/04_ART_BIBLE.md and docs/07_ACCEPTANCE.md. Use the current approved camera
prototype; main does not prescribe an angle, projection or pixel density.

## Review passes

1. Move the actor through every reachable region and elevation; no Sprite side edge.
2. Check feet, ground shadow, occlusion and depth against floors, stairs and walls.
3. Exercise all directions and actions; verify body, hands, weapon and VFX anchors.
4. Compare neutral, warm and cool lighting; preserve silhouette and identity colors.
5. Layer projectile, telegraph, impact and major feedback; danger remains readable.
6. Disable post-processing; core art and composition must still work.

Record editor evidence, runtime evidence and human acceptance separately. A clean
screenshot does not prove input response, animation stability or combat readability.
